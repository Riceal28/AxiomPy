# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in AxiomPy a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical tradeoffs.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith participation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and
other project-managed spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate, documented privately and respectful of affected reporters.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
available private contact or GitHub moderation tools. Do not open a public issue that
contains personal or sensitive details.

This policy is based on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
